# ASTON Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Status: DRAFT — requires completion of Annexes and legal/security approval before signature**  
**Version:** 0.1  
This DPA is intended for enterprise customers where ASTON processes Personal Data on the customer's documented instructions. It is not a substitute for the DPAs that Customer must separately enter into with Shopify, Cloudflare or other vendors where applicable.

## 1. Parties and relationship

This DPA is entered into between:

**Controller:** [CUSTOMER FULL LEGAL NAME], [jurisdiction], [address] ("Customer"); and  
**Processor:** [ASTON FULL LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [jurisdiction], [address] ("ASTON").

It supplements the [MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT / PILOT TERMS] dated [DATE] ("Main Agreement"). Customer is Controller and ASTON is Processor only for the processing described in Annex 1. Each party acts as an independent Controller for processing where it independently determines purposes and means, such as its own business contacts, billing, security and legal compliance.

If Customer itself acts as Processor for another Controller, Customer warrants it has authority to instruct ASTON and will identify that Controller and flow down applicable obligations.

## 2. Definitions and order of precedence

"Applicable Data Protection Law" includes, where applicable, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, Swiss data protection law, Argentina Law 25,326 and its applicable regulations, and other privacy laws applicable to the processing.

"Personal Data", "Processing", "Controller", "Processor", "Personal Data Breach" and "Data Subject" have the meanings given by applicable law.

For Personal Data processing, this DPA prevails over the Main Agreement in case of conflict. The Main Agreement otherwise remains in force.

## 3. Customer instructions and responsibilities

3.1 ASTON will process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including the Main Agreement, this DPA and agreed configuration, unless applicable law requires otherwise. ASTON will inform Customer of such legal requirement before processing unless prohibited.

3.2 Customer is responsible for the lawfulness, accuracy, quality and means of collection of Personal Data; providing notices; establishing a legal basis; obtaining necessary permissions; responding to Data Subject rights; and ensuring its instructions comply with law.

3.3 Customer must not provide special-category data, criminal-offence data, children's data, payment-card data, credentials, private keys or other high-risk data unless the parties expressly authorize the category and purpose in Annex 1 and agree additional safeguards in writing.

## 4. ASTON obligations

ASTON will:
- process Personal Data only for the documented purposes and duration in Annex 1;
- ensure persons authorized to process it are bound by confidentiality;
- implement appropriate technical and organizational measures described in Annex 2;
- provide reasonable assistance, taking account of the nature of processing, with Data Subject requests and obligations under Articles 32–36 GDPR where applicable;
- notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and provide information reasonably available to assist Customer's response;
- notify Customer if, in ASTON's reasonable opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law;
- make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and support audits under Section 9;
- at Customer's choice, return or delete Personal Data at the end of services, subject to legal retention obligations and secure backup cycles; and
- not sell Customer Personal Data or use it for ASTON's independent advertising purposes.

ASTON may process limited service telemetry for its own security, billing and service administration as an independent Controller where legally permitted; such data must be minimized and must not include Customer content unless necessary for security or support.

## 5. Security measures

ASTON will maintain measures appropriate to risk, which may include access control and least privilege, authentication controls, encryption in transit, secure configuration, logging, vulnerability management, backup and recovery, personnel confidentiality, incident response and secure deletion. The specific measures actually implemented for the contracted service must be documented in Annex 2. This DPA does not represent that ASTON holds ISO 27001, SOC 2, a penetration-test certification or any other certification unless separately evidenced in writing.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 Customer grants general written authorization for ASTON to engage subprocessors necessary to provide the services, subject to this section. ASTON will maintain a current subprocessor list identifying legal name, service, processing purpose and location, and will notify Customer of material additions or replacements at least [30] days in advance where practicable.

6.2 Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within [15] days after notice. The parties will work in good faith on a commercially reasonable alternative. If no reasonable alternative is available, Customer may terminate the affected service without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the terminated portion.

6.3 ASTON will impose written data protection obligations on each subprocessor no less protective in substance than this DPA and remains responsible for its subprocessors' performance to the extent required by law.

6.4 Potential infrastructure vendors, only if enabled in the actual deployment, may include Shopify for commerce/site operations and Cloudflare for application infrastructure, security, networking, storage or AI-related services. Their roles and applicable DPAs are governed by their own agreements. Any model provider, email, analytics, payment, support, logging or monitoring vendor must be added to the live subprocessor register before receiving Customer Personal Data. This draft does not assert that every listed service is currently enabled or that a vendor has accepted this DPA.

## 7. International transfers

ASTON will not transfer Personal Data subject to GDPR/UK GDPR to a third country unless a lawful transfer mechanism applies, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards under Article 46 GDPR. Where required, the parties will execute the applicable European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), select the correct module, complete annexes, conduct a transfer impact assessment and implement supplementary measures. The UK International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum or Swiss adaptations will be used where applicable.

The parties will document transfer locations, access, onward transfers and supplementary measures in Annex 3. A reference to a vendor's DPA does not automatically establish that every transfer in the ASTON service is lawful.

## 8. Data Subject requests and regulatory assistance

ASTON will promptly forward to Customer requests it receives that relate to Customer-controlled Personal Data, unless legally prohibited. ASTON will not respond on Customer's behalf except on documented instructions. ASTON will provide reasonable assistance with impact assessments, prior consultation and regulatory inquiries, taking into account the nature of processing and information available.

## 9. Audit and compliance information

On reasonable written request, ASTON will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. Where such information is insufficient, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality may conduct an audit no more than once per year, on reasonable notice during business hours, unless a Personal Data Breach or credible material non-compliance requires an additional audit. Audits must be scoped to the processing under this DPA, avoid access to other customers' data and not unreasonably disrupt operations. Each party bears its own costs unless the audit identifies material non-compliance attributable to ASTON.

## 10. Personal Data Breach

ASTON will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice will include, to the extent reasonably available, the nature of the breach, affected data categories and approximate scope, likely consequences, containment/remediation steps and a contact point. ASTON may provide information in phases as the investigation progresses. Customer remains responsible for determining and making notifications to supervisory authorities and Data Subjects where required by law.

## 11. Return and deletion

Upon termination or expiry, ASTON will, at Customer's choice, return or delete Customer Personal Data within [30] days, unless retention is required by law. Data in routine backups will be deleted or overwritten in accordance with the documented backup cycle and protected from further processing. ASTON may retain minimal records necessary for legal claims, security and compliance, subject to access restrictions and retention limits.

## 12. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the liability provisions of the Main Agreement, except to the extent such limitations are prohibited by applicable law or conflict with mandatory rights under applicable data protection law. The parties should obtain counsel review of liability caps, indemnities and regulatory exposure before execution.

## 13. Term and termination

This DPA remains in effect for as long as ASTON processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf. Termination of the Main Agreement terminates this DPA after completion of return/deletion obligations, without affecting provisions that by their nature survive.

## 14. Governing law

The governing law and dispute forum are those stated in the Main Agreement, subject to mandatory data protection law and the terms of any applicable Standard Contractual Clauses.

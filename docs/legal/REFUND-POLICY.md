This policy describes refunds for ASTON software, professional services and paid business pilots purchased directly from ASTON. If the Shopify storefront sells a different product or consumer subscription, this policy must be adapted to that specific offer before publication.

## 1. Business pilot services

For the standard 30-day B2B pilot priced at US$1,500, the scope, start date, deliverables, payment schedule and acceptance criteria are defined in the signed Order Form.

Unless the Order Form states otherwise:
- payment is due before kickoff;
- the pilot begins when cleared payment and required customer access/materials are received;
- fees attributable to services already performed and non-cancellable third-party costs are not refundable merely because Customer changes its mind;
- if ASTON cancels for convenience or materially fails to deliver the agreed scope and does not cure within a reasonable period after written notice, Customer will receive a proportionate refund for the undelivered portion; and
- if Customer terminates for convenience after kickoff, ASTON will calculate charges for work performed and committed costs and refund any remaining prepaid balance for undelivered work.

## 2. Digital products and subscriptions

Any separately offered digital product, license or subscription will have its own Order Form or checkout terms specifying the billing period, renewal, cancellation date and refund eligibility. No recurring subscription is created unless the customer affirmatively selects it.

Where access has been supplied or a digital service has begun, refund eligibility may depend on applicable law and the specific contract. ASTON will not exclude mandatory statutory rights.

## 5. Non-refundable amounts

To the extent permitted by applicable law, fees for completed services, consumed usage, bespoke work already delivered and non-cancellable third-party costs may be non-refundable. This section does not remove any refund, withdrawal, cancellation or other right that cannot legally be waived.

## 6. Consumer and mandatory rights

The B2B pilot is intended for business customers contracting in a commercial capacity. If ASTON offers goods or services to an individual acting as a consumer, mandatory consumer protection laws may provide additional rights, including withdrawal, cancellation, warranty or refund rights. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

For customers in Argentina, applicable mandatory consumer protection rules, including Law 24,240 where relevant, may override contractual wording. For EEA/UK consumers, applicable distance-selling and digital-content rules may apply. Eligibility and exceptions depend on the actual transaction and jurisdiction.

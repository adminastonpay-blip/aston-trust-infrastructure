## 1. Who is responsible

The entity identified above is the controller for personal data processed for its own website, sales, account administration, billing, security and business operations, unless a signed customer agreement identifies a different role for a particular processing activity.

Where ASTON processes personal data solely on documented instructions from an enterprise customer, ASTON acts as processor/service provider for that activity and the applicable Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs. A customer may separately act as controller of its end users' data.

Shopify, Cloudflare and other vendors may act as independent controllers for certain account, security, billing or service-improvement activities under their own notices, and as processors/subprocessors for other activities. Roles depend on the actual service and processing.

## 2. Personal data we may process

Depending on how you interact with ASTON, we may process:
- **Identity and contact data:** name, business email, telephone number, employer, role and correspondence.
- **Commercial data:** company, billing address, purchase/order records, contract and support history.
- **Website and device data:** IP address, device/browser identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, logs, pages viewed, timestamps and security events.
- **Account and authentication data:** account identifiers, access logs and authorization metadata. We do not ask you to send passwords or private keys through ordinary forms or email.
- **Service and attestation data:** agent identifiers, policy decisions, event metadata, evidence references, hashes, signatures and audit records generated or submitted when a customer uses ASTON.
- **Support and communications:** information you choose to include in enquiries, demo requests, incident reports or support tickets.

**Do not submit** payment card numbers, government identifiers, credentials, private cryptographic keys, special-category data, patient records, children's data, or other highly sensitive information unless ASTON has expressly agreed in writing to a suitable use case and safeguards.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We use personal data only for specified purposes, including:
- responding to enquiries, arranging demonstrations and negotiating or performing contracts;
- providing, securing, troubleshooting and supporting ASTON services;
- managing billing, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations;
- maintaining security logs, investigating abuse and protecting rights;
- sending relevant business communications where permitted; and
- improving services using appropriately minimized or aggregated information.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the legal basis appropriate to the activity: performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps; compliance with a legal obligation; legitimate interests balanced against individuals' rights; or consent where required. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing. Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law 25,326, processing will also be subject to the applicable requirements and rights.

We do not rely on consent where another legal basis is appropriate, and we do not use personal data for materially incompatible purposes without a lawful basis and required notice.

## 4. AI processing and safeguards

ASTON may use machine-learning or AI services to support agent workflows, policy evaluation, classification, security analysis or service functions. The exact model/provider, region, prompts, retention and training settings depend on the product configuration and must be disclosed in the applicable order form or service-specific notice.

We aim to minimize personal data sent to AI services. Customers should use synthetic, redacted or pseudonymized data for pilots and must not submit sensitive personal data unless expressly authorized in writing. We do not promise that AI outputs are error-free or that a model's output establishes the truth of an underlying claim.

ASTON will not knowingly authorize a vendor to train a general-purpose model on customer personal data unless the customer has expressly agreed in writing and the applicable law permits it. This statement is subject to verification against the actual provider terms and configuration before launch.

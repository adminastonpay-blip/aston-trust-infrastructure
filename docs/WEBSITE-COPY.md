## 3. Features Section

**Section headline**  
Three Building Blocks for Verifiable Agent Operations.

### Card 1 — Agent Ontology

**Title**  
A Shared Language for Agents.

**Body**  
ASTON's ontology structures the identities, roles, relationships, policies, and context surrounding AI agents. It helps teams reason about who an agent is, what it is allowed to do, and how its actions relate to the wider system.

### Card 2 — SafetyInterlock

**Title**  
Policy Before Execution.

**Body**  
SafetyInterlock is designed to place an explicit authorization gate before protected AI execution. When the required permit is absent or a policy check fails, the system is designed to fail closed rather than proceed by default.

### Card 3 — Cryptographic Evidence

**Title**  
Evidence You Can Independently Examine.

**Body**  
ASTON is designed to bind relevant events and attestations to cryptographic evidence, including hashes and digital signatures where supported. This makes records easier to verify and audit. A cryptographic proof can support integrity and provenance checks; it does not, by itself, prove that an underlying real-world event was true.
